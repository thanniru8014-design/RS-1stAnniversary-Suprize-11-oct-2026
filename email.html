My Law Letter to My Kanna. ❤️

My Kannalu, ❤️🥺

Kanna, I hope we can always speak to each other with patience and kindness, even on difficult days. I want us to feel safe sharing our feelings, listen to each other with open hearts, and remember that we're on the same team. Kanna, I don't need grand gestures or expensive gifts. I just want your love, patience, and care to come naturally every day. Even the little things mean a lot to me. Sometimes, all I need is for you to notice I'm sad, pull me close, and remind me that I'm not alone. ❤️

We've already faced so much pain, especially losing our little baby. 💔 Let's hold each other closer, protect each other's hearts, and never let anger or hurtful words push us apart. Let's choose happiness over anger, understand each other, support our dreams, and build a beautiful future together.

And Kanna, let's grow financially together too—support each other's careers, save wisely, make thoughtful decisions, and build a secure, peaceful future where we can fulfil our dreams side by side. I want us to celebrate not just our love, but every little milestone we achieve together. 💰🏡❤️

Kanna, nannu alone ga unchaku… I need you. Konni saarlu nenu strong ga kanipinchina, lopala nenu chala sensitive ga untanu. Naa feelings ni ardham chesukoni, naa side lo nilabadu. Please understand me and support me. When I cry, please be the one who wipes my tears, not the reason for them. 🥹❤️

Sometimes, even when you're beside me, I miss feeling close to you. I miss your hugs, your affection, and the comfort of knowing that your arms are my safest place. I wish you could understand the feelings behind my silence and hold me a little tighter when I'm hurting. I don't always need answers, Kanna. Sometimes, I just need YOU. 🫂❤️

But serious wife mode ON. 😌

When you said, “Nivalene nak peace lakunda potundi,” it hurt me deeply. Maybe you didn't mean it that way, but I couldn't forget those words. Please don't say them again, Kannalu. After everything we've been through, I want us to speak gently, even when we're angry, and never make each other feel alone. 💔❤️

Someday, when our little family grows, I want us to look back and smile at how much we learned and grew together. I want us to keep laughing, teasing each other, holding hands, supporting each other's dreams, and growing old together.

Kanna, if there's one thing I want you to remember, it's this: behind every little complaint is a heart that loves you deeply, and behind every tear is a wife who wishes she could feel your love a little more. I don't want us to lose the beautiful things we have because of temporary anger. I want to make memories with you, heal with you, and keep finding our way back to each other. ❤️

You're my home, my comfort, my Kanna, and my forever. Whatever life brings, I want us to face it side by side. When you see me, don't just see the person who sometimes gets upset; see the girl who chose you, who dreams of a lifetime with you, and who still wishes to feel safest in your arms.

So come here, Kannalu… give your wife one long hug. No arguments, no explanations, no ego — just you and me, holding each other a little closer. 🥹🫂❤️

I'll keep choosing YOU, every single time.

Always yours,
